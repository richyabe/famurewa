Full Biography

The Story Behind Lyceum Academy

Olumide J. Famurewa is a distinguished Science Educator and Clinical Psychologist with over a decade of experience helping students achieve academic excellence across multiple continents and curricula.

Currently serving as a Clinical Psychologist at the State Specialist Hospital, Osogbo, Mr. Famurewa brings a rare dual expertise to his teaching — combining rigorous scientific knowledge with deep psychological insight into how students learn, retain information, and perform under pressure.

As the founder of Lyceum Academy, he has successfully taught students from across the globe — including British (IGCSE/Cambridge), Nigerian (WAEC/NECO), American (SAT/Common Core), Canadian, Australian, Irish, South African, and Ghanaian curricula — making Lyceum one of the most internationally versatile tutoring platforms available.

His unique approach fuses scientific expertise with psychological principles — enabling learners to understand difficult concepts, overcome exam anxiety, build lasting confidence, and achieve outstanding academic results. Students don't just pass exams under his guidance; they develop a genuine love for learning.
